Our Blueprint for Building Apps & Games

// Studio playbook · Updated 19th September 2026

At BlankSlate Blueprint Core, every project starts the same way: a blank page, a clear goal, and a plan to ship.

This page walks through how we work — from the first sketch to the final release — and shows how a clean, structured foundation makes better products possible.

Our Development Process

Every product we build moves through four deliberate phases. No shortcuts, no guesswork.

  • Discover — We talk to the people who'll use it. What's the real problem?
  • Blueprint — We sketch the flow, the screens, and the core loop before writing a single line of code.
  • Build — We ship small, test fast, and iterate based on real feedback.
  • Launch — We polish, document, and release — then keep improving.

What We're Building Right Now

Our current slate spans two fronts: a focus app for indie developers and a browser-based puzzle game.

  • FocusForge — a lightweight productivity app for solo developers.
  • Core Loop — a browser puzzle game built with HTML5 canvas.

How We Work With Clients

We treat every engagement like a partnership. That means clear communication, realistic timelines, and honest trade-offs.
