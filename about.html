My Background

My name is Clinton Hamisi, and I'm the founder of BlankSlate Blueprint Core — a studio focused on building apps and games. I started this journey with a simple belief: that great software starts from a blank slate, guided by a clear blueprint.

I'm currently sharpening my web development skills through the IYF S12 program, and I spend most of my free time exploring how apps and games are built — from the first sketch to the final release.

My goal is simple: build products that are clean, fast, and genuinely useful — and help others do the same.
